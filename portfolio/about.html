Hi, I’m Anis Slimani. Welcome to a selection of my life’s work.

This portfolio collects projects shaped by academic research and personal curiosity in equal measure. Some began as coursework, others as the need to build, test, and express an idea. Together they show how I work: design as the place where strategy, visuals, technology, and storytelling meet.

I work across product design and product management, from brief to ship. Most recently I took an e-commerce platform from discovery to launch at Principotes, owning the roadmap alongside the full visual identity and a 28-screen design system. Before that, at Legalstart, I rolled out a design system across 100+ screens of the core incorporation flow and rebuilt the conversion funnels after a usability audit. I move comfortably between UX/UI, design systems, visual identity, motion, and front-end, and I use generative AI and MCP tooling to take design decisions all the way into code.
