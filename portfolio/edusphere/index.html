EduSphere

Home Website LinkedIn

In collaboration with Yeajin AHN, Wenting BAI and Mihai BRANGA-PEICU

EduSphere is an augmented reality educational tool that supports children’s learning through interactive 3D models and quizzes.

A structured user study compared AR-based learning with traditional paper-based methods, combining tasks, questionnaires, and semi-structured interviews with participants from the education field. The results showed AR improving engagement and information retention, confirming its value as a supplementary educational resource.
