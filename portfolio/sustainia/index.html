Sustainia

Home Website LinkedIn

SUSTAINIA is an educational serious game set in a dynamic cave environment, a continuation of Lascaux SHPELLË. Players take on roles such as Tour Guide, Energy Manager, and Environmental Specialist, collaborating to balance carbon emissions, resource management, and ecosystem restoration.

Through interactive gameplay, SUSTAINIA raises awareness about climate change, emphasising sustainable actions and their long-term impact while fostering critical thinking and teamwork.

In collaboration with Yeajin AHN and Jihyun PARK
