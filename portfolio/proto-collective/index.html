The PROTO Collective

Photo Exhibition features two distinct series exploring human interaction with architectural spaces.

“Through the Student’s Eyes:
Université Paris-Saclay”

Captures the campus through the lens of a student, using a Canon EOS 2000D to document its architectural forms and vibrant daily life from a personal perspective.

“Humanity in Brutalist Frames”

Shifts to the coexistence of human life and brutalist structures, reflecting resilience and alienation in the Anthropocene. Both series use the camera’s precision to highlight the interplay between humanity and the environments we shape and inhabit.
