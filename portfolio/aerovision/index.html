AeroVision

Home Website LinkedIn

AeroVision is an augmented reality experience design project inspired by aviation safety cards and videos.

Using Blender, Mixamo, and motion tracking, it builds dynamic 3D visuals and humanoid animations for stronger storytelling. Combining rigging, texturing, and audio editing, AeroVision explores AR techniques for immersive safety communication.
