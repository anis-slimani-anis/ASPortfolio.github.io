LET’S LEARN!

Home Website LinkedIn

LET’S LEARN! is a short animated film made in collaboration with Agathe Mornet, using 3D modeling techniques taught in the “Imageries numériques” course at EVMAN, Université Gustave Eiffel.

The project combines storytelling with digital artistry: animations crafted in Autodesk Maya, edited in VirtualDub and Adobe Premiere Pro. The featurette brings together technical skill and creative vision, showing the potential of 3D modeling for immersive visual storytelling.

In collaboration with Agathe MORNET
